# Product

## Register

product

## Users

YouTube viewers who organize channels into personal playlists and groups while browsing YouTube.

## Product Purpose

Add lightweight channel organization to YouTube without interrupting the native browsing workflow. Success means users can understand and change group membership directly where a channel appears.

## Brand Personality

Native, compact, unobtrusive.

## Anti-references

Avoid detached floating-card styling, decorative animation, excessive whitespace, and controls that visually compete with YouTube.

## Design Principles

- Preserve stable geometry while labels, counts, and selection state change.
- Update local disclosure state in place so the interface feels continuous.
- Communicate hierarchy through proximity and familiar controls.
- Match YouTube's surrounding density without copying unstable implementation details.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve keyboard operation, visible focus, practical pointer targets, sufficient contrast, and reduced-motion behavior.
